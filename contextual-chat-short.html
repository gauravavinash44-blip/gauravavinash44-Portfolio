Overview

Systematic is a US-based fintech platform for discovering, evaluating, and tracking companies.

It helps institutional and angel investors replace fragmented Excel workflows with a unified, data-driven system.

Problem

Investors had the data, but couldn’t act on it.

It wasn’t a data problem. It was a synthesis problem. The interface demanded precision before investors had even formed their thesis.

  • Search was too rigid — keyword-only, with no intent matching.
  • Filters demanded expertise they didn’t have: 40+ undifferentiated parameters.
  • Evaluating a single company took 45 to 90 minutes across 5 to 7 tabs.
  • Nearly three in four research sessions ended with no shortlist, no action.

Solution

Three directions were on the table. We weighed all three on impact versus effort.

Enhanced filters

Low effort and familiar, but they didn’t fix the core problem. Investors still needed upfront knowledge to use them right.

AI recommendation dashboard

High potential impact, but it risked cold-start failure for new investors and felt like opaque “magic.” Skeptical institutional users wouldn’t trust it right away. We parked this for Phase 2.

Conversational interface

High impact, and it handled ambiguous intent naturally. It met investors at the exploration stage instead of after. Higher engineering complexity, but it hit the biggest friction point, so we went with it.

→ We built Conversational Discovery first, since it addressed the earliest and biggest drop-off point.

The AI Insights Engine came second, once discovery was fixed — targeting the next-highest friction point at the evaluation stage.

Output

  • Chat interface where investors type natural language queries instead of fighting with filters
  • Transparent loading states — when the AI is pulling from a data source, the loading bar shows it’s connecting and fetching, so investors see where the answer is coming from, not a black box
  • AI-summarized company profiles that surface key signals instead of making investors read the whole page
  • Every AI claim links back to its original source — this source-linking was the main thing that built trust with a skeptical, data-literal audience

Interaction Details

Micro-interactions that removed friction without stealing attention.

Chat expansion behavior

Assistant opens progressively to preserve focus and avoid layout shock in dense data views.

Prompt chips + dual-mode

Users can move between classic search and AI mode without losing work-in-progress context.

Notification states

Subtle status updates communicate model progress and confidence without noisy interruptions.

A 2-hour research workflow compressed to about 35 minutes.
