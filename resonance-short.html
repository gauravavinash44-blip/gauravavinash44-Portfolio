Overview

What if Spotify knew which part of the song you were actually after?

Weekend trip. Friends. Someone picks a track, waits through the intro, then drags the scrubber to the bit everyone already knows. Same move, song after song.

Resonance is a speculative playback option that lives inside Spotify’s existing Now Playing — not a new feed, not a chatbot, not a separate destination. One capability on play.

Problem

People don’t always love the whole song. They love a moment.

  • A lyric. A bassline. A drop. A vocal run. A build they always replay.
  • Different listeners can want completely different seconds of the same track.
  • There’s no universal “best part.” There can be a best moment for a particular listener.
  • The friction isn’t discovery. It’s dragging the timeline again when you already know where you’re going.

Opportunity

From “songs you like” to “moments you respond to.”

Spotify already learns from skips, seeks, replays, saves, and returns. Resonance explores stretching that learning inside the track — lyrics, vocals, beat, bass, energy, structure, plus how you actually behave.

Song understanding + listening behaviour = a personalised start. Collective signals can help (“2.5K listeners started here”) without pretending that’s everyone’s favourite.

Principle

Spotify, with one smarter play — not a new app.

Inside Now Playing

Orbit icon beside title and add. Not inside shuffle. Not a new tab.

AI stays quiet

Communicate “understands how you listen,” not “AI is analysing your music.”

Personal vs popular

“Your moment.” “Most replayed.” No P1/P2/P3 in the UI.

Listener decides

On, off, skip, seek, start from zero. Suggest, don’t commandeer.

Solution

A short path from first open to an active Resonance start.

Splash→ Home→ Bottom-sheet intro→ Now Playing→ Icon on→ Moment start
First-time education

Home sheet: Resonance, short benefit, Got it. Now Playing gets a small tooltip on the icon — not another sheet.

Activation motion

Dot merges into the ring, icon goes Spotify green. ~500ms. No sparkles, no bounce.

Timeline markers

Subtle dots for meaningful moments. Tap for natural labels: your moment, popular, listener counts.

Preference + skip nudge

Optional “what do you wait for?” multi-select. Repeated skips can ask: Looking for a moment?

Reflection

What this exploration is arguing for.

  • Personalisation can go one layer deeper than the track list — into time inside the song.
  • Confidence should be honest: your moment, popular moment, or just explore.
  • The interesting design problem isn’t the model. It’s keeping Spotify familiar while the start position quietly gets smarter.

Song + listener + moment = personalised playback.
